## User story
As a family member, I want to group photos into albums (e.g. "Summer 2026"), so that I can find related photos together.

## Acceptance criteria (draft)
- **AC-1** I can create, rename and delete albums. Deleting an album does not delete its images.
- **AC-2** I can add images to an album and remove them from it. One image can be in several albums.
- **AC-3** The album page shows its images in the same grid and viewer as the gallery (REQ-003, REQ-004).
- **AC-4** I can choose a cover image for an album.

## Open questions
- Who may edit an album: its creator only, or every member?
